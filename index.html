Every newcomer plays by its own rules

A block puzzle that fights back.

Drag blocks onto the 8x8 board, fill a row or a column to clear it, and keep the board open for as long as you can. Then the garden wakes up.

The siege

A garden under siege

The bugs do not wait. The first flowers and the first bees show up within a minute, and something new arrives every few stages, each with its own rules.

  • Tap, triple-tap or slash: every bug is stopped its own way
  • Every newcomer gets its own reveal
  • The Bug Book keeps track of the ones you have met

Boss fights

Beat the Beetle King

Every 10 stages the one who sends the pests shows up himself. Clear lines to hurt him before his attack timer runs out.

  • He shows up every 10 stages
  • Beat his attack timer by clearing lines
  • Win coins and a free power-up

Grow

Grow flowers, earn coins

Flower blocks arrive as buds. Clear a line beside one to water it, and watch it grow from bud to bloom to big bloom.

  • Harvest at the right moment for the biggest reward
  • Wait too long and it wilts
  • Place blocks quickly to fill the heat bar: up to x3 points

Dress it up

100 things to collect

Dress the game up your way: board frames, block faces, hats for the Beetle King, garden ornaments and blade trails.

  • Board frames, block faces and blade trails
  • Hats for the Beetle King and garden ornaments
  • Own six faces or six hats and switch on Randomize for a different look every time
